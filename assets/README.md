# Camaro Project Website Assets Directory

Place your project photos in this directory. 

To link your photos to specific work logs on the website, name your image files as follows and save them in this folder:

- **The Story Log**: `birthday_bow.jpg`
- **Drivetrain Overhaul Log**: `drivetrain.jpg`
- **LED Headlights Conversion Log**: `headlights.jpg`
- **Steering Wheel Wrap Log**: `steering_wheel.jpg`
- **Stereo & Cameras Log**: `radio_cameras.jpg`
- **Suspension Lowering Springs Log**: `suspension.jpg`
- **Miami Summer Maintenance Log**: `cooling_tuneup.jpg`
- **Ignition Tune-Up Log**: `ignition_tuneup.jpg`
- **2026 Spring Readiness Log**: `oil_change_2026.jpg`

Once you place your photos here with these filenames, they will automatically override the vector schematics and display inside the corresponding log cards and thread modals!
